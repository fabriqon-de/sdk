# Code of Conduct

Fabriqon welcomes constructive participation regardless of experience, background, identity or ability. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise and patient. Discuss code and ideas without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, third-party work and the limits of what contributors can share.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted personal contact and publishing another person's private information.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Repeated disruption or personal abuse is not.

## Report a concern

For a private report, use the contact details in [Fabriqon's legal notice](https://fabriqon.de/impressum.html) and request a suitable private channel. Do not publish identifying details or private correspondence in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request edits, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Actions should be proportionate to severity and repeated behavior. Reporters' privacy should be respected; confidentiality or fixed response times cannot be guaranteed.
